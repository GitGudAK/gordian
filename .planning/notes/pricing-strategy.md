# Gordian pricing (decided)

- Position: value tier under the Calm/Headspace $69.99/yr wellness ceiling, above junk tier.
- Rationale: App Store price raises on subs force consent churn (one-way door) → launch high side;
  discounting later is free (offer codes, intro offers, silent decreases).
- Unit economics: <$0.01/session (two-tier gate), CF free plan, 15% Apple cut via SBP → ~85%+ margin.
- Mix model: 1,000 payers at 55/35/10 annual/monthly/lifetime ≈ $36–40K ARR.
- Psychology: annual anchored between monthly and lifetime; charm pricing; trial-wall
  personalization planned ("You untied N knots this week" — proxy already counts).
- Optimization inside App Store constraints: offer-code cohorts as discount lab (REDDIT-LAUNCH etc.),
  PPO for page tests, intro offer BEFORE any base-price cut, regional tiers only on demand signal.
- Triggers: <2% trial-completer conversion after 500 trials → intro offer test.
  Monthly >70% of new payers → raise monthly to $5.99 (not lower annual).
  Lifetime >25% of revenue → raise lifetime to $79.99.
- Parked: corporate/teams tier (needs seats/admin; Phase 2 conversation).
