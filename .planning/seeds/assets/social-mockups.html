Design review · seeds 3 & 4

Decisions become social objects

Two features, one strategy at different depths. The Verdict Card makes a single decision shareable; Shared Knots makes the deciding itself something two people do together. Mockups below use the shipped design system — nothing here invents new visual language.

The announcement artifact. Decision loud, brand quiet, dilemma absent by default — people announce the cut, not the deliberation. Renders on-device (ImageRenderer), story and square variants. ~1 week, zero server.

Shared Knots

One dilemma, two guts, same frozen sixty seconds — no peeking, reveal only when both have cut. The invite is the growth loop; the reveal is the product. Ephemeral server state, token links, still no accounts. 3–6 weeks.
