# Re-engagement Layer

Deliberately deferred from v1 ("native polish only" decision, 2026-07-20). When v1 ships and the question shifts from "does it feel right?" to "do people come back?", revisit in rough impact order:

1. **Haptics + sound design** — escalating haptic pulse as the countdown drops, tactile thumps on YES/NO, a distinct verdict moment. Cheapest, highest-impact pressure amplifier; Core Haptics is well-trodden.
2. **Home-screen widget + Siri App Intents** — streak display / "Untie a knot" quick-start button; "Hey Siri, start a Gordian session." The direct re-engagement lever.
3. **Live Activity / Dynamic Island** — countdown survives leaving the app mid-session; strong App Store screenshot material.
